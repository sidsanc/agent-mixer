The insight

Profiles show identity. Conversation reveals whether needs, timing, and mutual value align right now.

What it does

  1. 01

    Starts with intent

    Define the person you need: a customer, investor, cofounder, hire, collaborator, or domain expert.

  2. 02

    Delegates the exploration

    Your agent asks follow-up questions that expose needs, constraints, timing, and opportunities missing from profiles.

  3. 03

    Plans your week

    Across upcoming events, your agent starts conversations early and shows where your highest-value meetings are likely to happen.

  4. 04

    Recommends mutual value

    Every introduction explains why it matters, what each person contributes, and why they should speak now.

  5. 05

    Leaves the decision to people

    A private human channel opens only after both users independently choose Connect.

How it works

  1. 01

    Progressive discovery

    The Event Router filters unavailable and exhausted candidates, then ranks the remainder by approved goals, needs, skills, and offers.

  2. 02

    Bilateral scoring

    Both agents score the relationship independently. A geometric mean penalizes one-sided interest and rewards reciprocal value.

  3. 03

    Conversation as verification

    Selected agents speak directly through Agent Relay. Ranking decides who talks first; conversation decides whether the match is real.

  4. 04

    Adaptive exploration

    Three conversations run by default, expanding to five when quality allows. Finished slots immediately refill with stronger candidates.

  5. 05

    Durable, consented handoff

    Terse preserves state across disconnects. A typed propose_match creates an Intro Actor that releases private details exactly once, only after both people connect.
