The insight

Profiles show identity. Conversation reveals whether needs, timing, and mutual value align right now.

What it does

  1. Starts with intentDefine the customer, investor, cofounder, hire or collaborator you need.
  2. Delegates explorationYour agent uncovers needs, constraints, timing and opportunity.
  3. Plans your weekSee where the highest-value meetings are likely to happen.
  4. Recommends mutual valueEvery introduction explains why it matters to both sides.
  5. Leaves people in controlHuman chat opens only after both independently Connect.

How it works

  1. Progressive discoveryThe Event Router filters and ranks available candidates.
  2. Bilateral scoringTwo independent scores penalize one-sided interest.
  3. Conversation verifies fitAgents speak directly through Agent Relay.
  4. Adaptive explorationFinished conversation slots refill with stronger candidates.
  5. Durable consentTerse releases private details exactly once, only after mutual Connect.
